About the Leave Management System

Designed specifically for VMR Pradeep Kumar Institution of Engineering and Technology, this system replaces manual paper-based leave slips with a robust, automated digital workflow.

•
Automated Accounting: Instant calculation of leave days and dynamic deduction upon HOD approval.
•
Conflict & Overlap Detection: Prevents double-booking dates and guarantees balance integrity.
•
Role-Based Security: Strict backend authorization separating Faculty operations from HOD administrative approvals.
•
Transparent Workflow: Direct visibility of submission dates, decision timestamps, and reviewer remarks.
